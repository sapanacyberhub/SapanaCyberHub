01 Program Overview

The SapanaCyberHub Partner Network ("Program") allows approved individuals and creators ("Partners") to promote SapanaCyberHub products — including SCH X Listen and SCH X LoL — and earn rewards based on user growth and platform revenue.

By applying to, being approved for, or participating in the Program, you agree to these Terms in full. If you disagree with any part, do not participate.

02 Eligibility

  • You must be at least 18 years of age.
  • You must have a valid SapanaCyberHub account in good standing.
  • You must have at least one active, publicly verifiable promotion channel (Instagram, YouTube, Telegram, WhatsApp group, Facebook page, etc.).
  • Employees, contractors, or immediate family members of SapanaCyberHub are not eligible.
  • Partners must not have been previously banned or removed from the Program.

03 Partner Rewards — Simplified

We keep the reward structure simple and transparent. As an approved Partner you receive exactly three types of rewards:

The Three Rewards:
🎨 Creator Credit  ·  💰 Weekly Revenue Share  ·  ⚡ Boost Token
WHAT YOU DOWHAT YOU GET
Every 10 active users you bring1 Creator Credit (LoL posting)

Creator Credit: Credited to your LoL account automatically when your 10th, 20th, 30th (and so on) referred user becomes active. "Active" means they have logged in and completed at least one engagement action in the past 7 days.

Weekly Revenue Share: Every Sunday, 1% of the previous week's platform ad revenue is distributed among all active Partners in descending order by total referred users — meaning Partners with more users receive a proportionally larger share. Partners with zero active referred users in that week receive nothing.

Boost Token: Automatically applied to your next new LoL post when you hit the 1,000 referred users milestone. Extends that post's earning window from 48 hours to 72 hours. One token per 1,000-user milestone increment (1,000 / 2,000 / 3,000…).

⚠️ No Guarantee: Revenue pool amounts vary week to week based on platform earnings. SapanaCyberHub makes no guarantee of minimum payout amounts.

04 Referral Tracking

Each approved Partner receives a unique Partner ID and a referral URL in the format:

https://sapanacyberhub.com/signup?ref=YOUR_PARTNER_ID
  • When a new user visits your referral link, their device stores your Partner ID locally (localStorage) for up to 30 days.
  • When they create an account within that window, your Partner ID is permanently saved against their profile.
  • Existing users who already have an account cannot be re-attributed to a partner.
  • Self-referral (referring yourself or your own secondary accounts) is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate termination.
  • Only genuine unique human users count toward your metrics. Bot traffic, incentivised fake signups, or duplicate accounts are detected and excluded automatically.

05 Prohibited Conduct

The following are strictly forbidden and will result in immediate termination and forfeiture of all earned rewards:

  • Creating fake accounts or using bots to inflate referral counts.
  • Paying or incentivising users with cash/gifts to sign up (unless explicitly approved as a campaign by SapanaCyberHub).
  • Misleading users about the nature or earnings potential of SapanaCyberHub products.
  • Spamming in groups, comments, or DMs without permission.
  • Using the SapanaCyberHub name, logo, or branding in any way not approved in writing.
  • Promoting on platforms that violate that platform's own terms of service.
  • Sharing your Partner ID or referral link in exchange for money.
