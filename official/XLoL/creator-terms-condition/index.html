03 — Community Guidelines

Community
Guidelines

SCH × LoL is a laugh-first community. These guidelines protect creators, viewers, and the platform's integrity. Violations result in content removal, credit forfeiture, or permanent ban.

The Golden Rule: Post content you'd be comfortable sharing under your real name and that you'd be fine with all ages seeing. If it passes that test — it probably belongs here.

✅3.1 — What We Welcome
  • ✓ Original funny videos, memes, GIFs, and images you created or have explicit rights to use.
  • ✓ Relatable comedy — everyday situations, harmless fails, clever edits.
  • ✓ Clean roasts and parody that do not target real individuals maliciously.
  • ✓ Satire of ideas, trends, or public concepts — not private individuals.
  • ✓ Regional language content in any Indian language — diversity is celebrated.
  • ✓ Collaborative shout-outs and reactions (with original creator credit in description).
⚠️3.2 — Content That Requires Extra Care
  • ⚠ Satirical political content — keep it about policies, not personal attacks on individuals.
  • ⚠ Mild profanity — context matters; gratuitous abuse is not comedy.
  • ⚠ Dark humour — must not glorify real harm, death, or suffering.
  • ⚠ Reaction content — must include your own commentary; do not re-upload others' content wholesale.
🚫3.3 — Strictly Prohibited Content

The following content will be immediately removed and may result in account termination and earnings forfeiture:

  • ✗ Copyright Infringement: Copyrighted music, movie/TV clips, brand logos, or any content you do not own the rights to — including background audio from Bollywood, Hollywood, or any commercial source without explicit licence.
  • ✗ Sexual Content: Nudity, sexually explicit imagery or audio, sexual innuendo targeting real individuals. Any content sexualising minors carries absolute zero tolerance — immediate permanent ban and mandatory reporting to authorities.
  • ✗ Violence & Gore: Graphic violence, real-world injury footage, torture, animal cruelty, or content glorifying self-harm or suicide.
  • ✗ Hate Speech: Content attacking individuals or groups based on religion, caste, gender, sexuality, ethnicity, disability, or national origin. Comedy that punches down on marginalised communities.
  • ✗ Harassment & Bullying: Targeted harassment of private individuals, doxxing, threatening language, or coordinated attacks on specific users.
  • ✗ Misinformation: Health misinformation, election fraud claims, fabricated news presented as real, or deepfakes designed to deceive.
  • ✗ Illegal Activity: Drug promotion, weapons, gambling, fraudulent schemes, or any content facilitating illegal acts under Indian law.

  • ✗ Impersonation: Pretending to be a celebrity, public figure, SapanaCyberHub team member, or another SCH member.
  • ✗ Private Information: Sharing another person's private photos, location, phone number, or identifying details without their explicit consent.
🎵3.4 — Copyright & Audio — Critical Note

Using copyrighted audio is one of the most common policy violations. Key rules:

  • Do not upload clips from movies, TV shows, or streaming platforms (Netflix, Prime, etc.).

04 — Creator Monetization

How Creators
Earn

SCH × LoL runs a transparent engagement-based monetization model. Your earnings are a function of the real engagement your content generates — not follower count, not virality luck. Here is exactly how it works.

🔓4.1 — Eligibility to Create & Post

To publish a LoL post you must satisfy both of the following simultaneously:

🔥 LoL Streak 5 Days minimum
🪙 LoL Creator Credits 1 Credit minimum
  • LoL Streak: Log in and interact with the feed for 5 consecutive days. Missing a single day resets the streak back to zero.
  • LoL Creator Credit (LoL CC): Earned by accumulating 1,000 engagement score points through viewing, liking, and sharing other creators' content.
  • Posting costs 1 LoL CC, deducted from your balance upon successful post submission.
  • These requirements ensure only genuinely active community members become creators — protecting feed quality for all viewers.
⚡4.2 — Engagement Score & Credit System

Every interaction you perform generates personal engagement score, which drives Creator Credit accumulation. When viewers interact with your posts, that engagement contributes to your post's earning calculation.

Action You TakePersonal Engagement PointsCredits Milestone
👁 Viewing a post (5+ seconds)+10 pointsEvery 1,000 points = 1 LoL CC
💖 Liking a post+20 points
ᯓ➤ Sharing a post+3 points

Your Profile dashboard shows your current progress bar toward the next Creator Credit (0 / 1000).

🎁4.3 — Session Bonus Rewards

As you scroll and engage during a session, you accumulate a session engagement score. When this score crosses a threshold (50+ points) and you have swiped enough cards, a Bonus Card appears offering a possible Listen Coin reward.

  • Tapping "Claim" opens a sponsor offer in a new tab and submits your session data for server-side reward calculation.
  • Rewards are not guaranteed for every session visit — they are server-side decisions based on sponsor availability and session quality. This is always disclosed on the Bonus Card itself.
  • Skipping the Bonus Card carries no penalty whatsoever.
  • Any Listen Coins awarded appear in your SCH × Listen Wallet, typically within a few minutes.
⏱️4.4 — Post Lifespan & Earning Window
  • Every LoL post is live for 48 hours from the moment of publishing.
  • Your post earns estimated revenue during its 48-hour live window based on viewer engagement.
  • After 48 hours the post status changes to "Done" and no further engagement is credited to it.
  • Final earnings per post are visible in Profile → Post History → tap any post thumbnail.
  • Deleting a live post forfeits its remaining earning potential for that window.
📊4.5 — Daily LoL Leaderboard

The Daily LoL Leaderboard ranks all creators by aggregate engagement score for the current calendar day. It resets every 24 hours. Creator IDs (not full names or emails) are displayed publicly on the leaderboard to all authenticated LoL users. Top 3 positions receive enhanced feed visibility; there is currently no additional direct monetary reward for leaderboard placement.

📢4.6 — Advertising & Revenue Model

SCH × LoL is entirely ad-supported. Ad revenue from Monetag and Adsterra networks funds creator earnings. The following ad types may appear in the feed:

  • Banner Ads: Adsterra display banners within sponsored feed cards (auto-sized to your device).
  • Native Ads: Sponsored content cards that blend naturally with the LoL feed.
  • Vignette Ads: Monetag overlay ads — appear briefly and can be closed.
  • Quick-Break Cards: Scheduled sponsor breaks appearing every 5–7 swipes on average.
  • Smart Link Cards: Tap-only sponsor links — these never auto-redirect your browser.

User Protection Guarantee: No ad on SCH × LoL will ever auto-redirect your browser without your explicit tap or click. All redirect-capable ads require deliberate user interaction.

🚨4.7 — Earnings Forfeiture Conditions

Estimated earnings may be partially or fully forfeited if:

  • Your post is removed for violating Community Guidelines or copyright policy.
  • Engagement on your post is determined to be artificially inflated (bots, coordinated like-rings, etc.).
  • Your account is suspended or banned before the transfer threshold is reached.
  • You delete a post while it is still within its 48-hour earning window.

Engagement score calculations performed by SapanaCyberHub Cloud Functions are final. There is no appeal process for score adjustments resulting from enforcement action.

05 — Withdrawal & Wallet

Earnings &
Withdrawal Flow

LoL earnings do not go directly to your bank account. They flow through a structured two-stage process — first into your SCH × Listen Wallet, then you can withdraw from there. Every step is explained below.

Why two stages? SCH × LoL is an extension of SCH × Listen. Your Listen Wallet is the single verified payout identity for all SapanaCyberHub earning sub-platforms. This prevents fraud, ensures KYC compliance, and keeps withdrawal management unified in one place you already trust.

Stage 1 — LoL Earnings → Listen Wallet

1

Earn on the Platform

Post content, receive engagement (views, likes, shares from the audience). Your post's engagement converts into an Estimated Earning shown in ₹ on your Profile dashboard.

2

Reach the Transfer Threshold

Your total accumulated estimated earnings across all posts must reach ₹2,500 before a transfer is permitted. This prevents micro-transactions that carry disproportionate processing costs.

3

Platform Fee Is Deducted

Upon transfer, a 30% platform fee is applied to your gross LoL earnings. This fee covers: ad network payouts, Firebase infrastructure, Creator Credit system, content moderation, and platform development. You receive the remaining 70%.

4

Funds Land in Listen Wallet

The net amount (70% of gross) is credited to your SCH × Listen Wallet. Your LoL estimated earning counter resets to ₹0. A full record appears in your Profile → Transfer History.

5

One Transfer Per Calendar Month

You may initiate only one LoL→Listen transfer per calendar month. If you have already transferred in the current month, the Transfer button shows a "claimed this month" message and locks until the next month begins.

Min. to Transfer ₹2,500
Platform Fee 30%
You Receive 70%
Frequency Once / Month

Stage 2 — Listen Wallet → Your Bank Account

🏦Bank Withdrawal via SCH × Listen

Once funds are in your SCH × Listen Wallet, withdrawal to your bank account is handled entirely through the SCH × Listen platform — not through SCH × LoL. The withdrawal rules, minimum amounts, KYC requirements, and processing times are governed by the SCH × Listen Terms and Withdrawal Policy.

How to withdraw: Open SCH × Listen → Wallet → Withdraw. Your LoL-transferred balance will be included in your total Listen Wallet balance available for bank withdrawal.

SCH × LoL has no visibility into or control over the bank withdrawal process once funds leave the LoL system into your Listen Wallet. All bank withdrawal queries must be directed to SCH × Listen support.

💡Illustrative Example

Say you have accumulated ₹3,000 in LoL estimated earnings and initiate a transfer:

  • Platform fee (30%) = ₹900 retained by SapanaCyberHub
  • Net transferred to Listen Wallet = ₹2,100
  • Your Listen Wallet balance increases by ₹2,100
  • Your LoL estimated earning resets to ₹0
  • You can now request a bank withdrawal of ₹2,100 (subject to Listen's withdrawal minimum) from within SCH × Listen
