Is Your Phone Hacked? 21 Warning Signs & Protection Guide (2025)

SapanaCyberHub Cyber Lab

Phone hacking is rising rapidly in India. Hackers use spyware, stalkerware, remote screen apps, SIM swap attacks, and WhatsApp-based tricks to take control of your mobile. This guide helps you **detect, confirm, remove, and secure your phone** from hacking attempts.

On this page: Warning Signs Your Phone is Hacked How to Confirm if Your Phone is Hacked How to Remove Hackers from Your Phone How to Protect Your Phone from Future Hacks Secret Codes to Detect Tracking More Cyber Guides

1. 21 Warning Signs Your Phone is Hacked

If 4 or more signs match → **HIGH probability** your phone is hacked or monitored.
  • Battery draining extremely fast
  • Phone heats even when idle
  • Apps opening by themselves
  • Unknown apps installed
  • Microphone activates randomly
  • Strange clicking sounds during calls
  • Frequent pop-ups
  • High mobile data usage
  • Messages marked "read" automatically
  • Random notifications disappear instantly
  • Phone restarts automatically
  • WhatsApp numbers blocked without you doing it
  • Your private information leaked
  • Google account activity from unknown devices
  • Screen lights up with no notifications
  • Contacts receive spam from your phone
  • Typing delay or screen freezing
  • Rapid battery loss during calls
  • Unrecognized Bluetooth pairings
  • Strange banking/UPI alerts

2. How to Confirm If Your Phone Is Hacked

✓ Check Device Admin Apps

Settings → Security → Device Admin Apps
Remove anything suspicious.

✓ Check Accessibility Permissions

Settings → Accessibility → Installed Services
Unknown apps with full control = spyware.

✓ Check Usage Stats

Settings → Digital Wellbeing → App Usage
Spy apps show high background usage.

✓ Check Screen Recording Apps

Look for:
  • VRecorder
  • Mobizen
  • QuickSupport
  • AnyDesk

✓ Check Unknown Profiles (iPhone)

Settings → General → VPN & Device Management

3. How to Remove Hackers from Your Phone

Step 1 — Remove Device Admin Rights

Settings → Security → Device Admin → Disable

Step 2 — Uninstall Suspicious Apps

Step 3 — Remove Hidden Malware

Settings → Apps → Show System Apps

Step 4 — Reset Network Settings

Breaks remote access.

Step 5 — Full Factory Reset (If heavily hacked)

Do NOT restore from old backup — it may re-install spyware.

4. How to Protect Your Phone from Future Hacks

  • Enable SIM Lock
  • Block unknown app installation
  • Use strong phone password
  • Enable WhatsApp 2FA
  • Enable Google advanced protection
  • Never install apps from Telegram/WhatsApp
  • Do not share your phone with others
  • Avoid screen-sharing apps
  • Keep phone updated

5. Secret Codes to Detect Tracking

  • *#21# — Call/SMS diversion check
  • *#62# — Check forwarding
  • *#*#4636#*#* — Device & network status
  • *#*#197328640#*#* — Monitoring mode
If call forwarding is ON without your permission → your phone is likely tapped.
