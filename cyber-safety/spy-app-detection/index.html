Spy App Detection 2025 — How to Find Hidden Tracking Apps

SapanaCyberHub Cyber Safety Guide

Spy apps (stalkerware) secretly track your messages, location, calls, WhatsApp, Instagram, and everything you do. This guide teaches you how to **detect**, **remove**, and **block** spy apps using simple 2025 methods that actually work.

On this page: Signs Your Phone Has a Spy App How to Check for Spy Apps Secret Codes to Detect Tracking How to Remove Spy Apps Secure Your Phone After Removing Spy Apps More Cyber Guides

1. Signs Your Phone Has a Spy App Installed

If 3 or more signs match → HIGH CHANCE your phone is being tracked.
  • Battery draining unusually fast
  • Phone heating even when not used
  • Unknown apps in settings
  • Data usage increasing rapidly
  • Screen lights up randomly
  • Echo or clicking sound during calls
  • WhatsApp/Instagram opening automatically
  • Stranger knows your private info

2. How to Check for Spy Apps (Hidden Apps Detection)

✓ Method 1 — Check “Device Admin Apps”

Settings → Security → Device Admin Apps
Remove apps like:
  • Service App
  • System Helper
  • Mobile Tracker
  • Update Service

✓ Method 2 — Check Accessibility Services

Settings → Accessibility → Installed Services
If unknown apps have “Full Control”, it is a spy app.

✓ Method 3 — Check Hidden App List

Dialer → *#*#225#*#*

(Shows hidden apps on some devices)

✓ Method 4 — Check Device Usage Stats

Settings → Digital Wellbeing → App Usage
Unknown apps with high hourly usage = spy apps.

3. Secret Codes to Detect Spy & Tracking

  • *#21# — Check call forwarding
  • *#62# — See where missed calls/SMS are redirected
  • *#*#4636#*#* — Phone information + active connections
  • *#*#197328640#*#* — Diagnose network monitoring
If call/SMS forwarding is ON → someone may be spying on your phone.

4. How to Remove Spy Apps Completely

Step 1 — Disable Device Admin Access

Settings → Security → Device Admin Apps → Disable

Step 2 — Uninstall Suspicious Apps

Step 3 — Remove Unknown Profiles (iPhone)

Settings → General → VPN & Device Management

Step 4 — Reset Network Settings

Removes remote access

Step 5 — Factory Reset (If heavily compromised)

Backup data before factory reset. NEVER restore from infected backup.

5. Secure Your Phone After Removing Spy Apps

  • Enable app lock for WhatsApp & Instagram
  • Enable SIM Lock
  • Use strong phone password
  • Disable unknown app installation
  • Avoid giving your phone to others
  • Do not install clone/modified apps
