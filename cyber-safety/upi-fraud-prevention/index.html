UPI Fraud Prevention 2025 — Stop Fake Payments & Money Loss

SapanaCyberHub Cyber Safety Guide

UPI fraud is now India’s fastest-growing cyber scam. Criminals use fake payments, refund scams, UPI requests, QR-code traps, WhatsApp calls, and screen-sharing tricks to steal money instantly.

On this page: Most Important UPI Rule Latest UPI Scam Types 2025 How to Protect Your UPI If You Got Scammed Messages Banks NEVER Send More Cyber Safety Guides

1. The MOST Important UPI Rule (That Saves 99% Indians)

To receive money → You NEVER need UPI PIN or QR scan.

Only sending money requires PIN.

If someone tells you to:

  • Scan QR to receive money
  • Enter UPI PIN for cashback
  • Approve a collect request to get refund

→ IT IS 100% A SCAM.

2. Latest UPI Scams in 2025 (Real Indian Fraud Patterns)

1) Fake UPI Collect Request Scam

Scammer pretends to be:

  • Buyer
  • Refund agent
  • Electricity/Phone company
  • Customer support
Approving the request sends YOUR money to them instantly.

2) Fake Payment SMS / Screenshot Scam

Scammer sends messages like:

“₹5,000 credited to your account.”

No money is actually received.

3) QR Code Refund Scam

They claim scanning a QR will “credit refund”.

All QR codes are for SENDING money — never receiving.

4) Screen Sharing Scam

Apps used:

  • AnyDesk
  • TeamViewer
  • QuickSupport

They watch you enter PINs and OTPs in real time.

5) Job / Task / Survey Scam

No real job asks for money to “activate your account”.

6) KYC Suspension Scam

Fake SMS:

“Your KYC expired. Account will be blocked in 24 hours.”

7) Customer Support Scam

Fake helpline numbers trick users into transferring money.

3. How to Protect Your UPI (2025 Protection Checklist)

✓ Rule 1: Only enter UPI PIN when YOU are sending money.

✓ Rule 2: Decline unknown UPI collect requests.

✓ Rule 3: Uninstall screen-sharing apps unless necessary.

✓ Rule 4: Set lowest possible UPI daily limit.

✓ Rule 5: Lock UPI apps with biometrics.

✓ Rule 6: Never call numbers from Google search.

Most “customer care” numbers online are FAKE.

✓ Rule 7: Check UPI device list often.

Google Pay → Settings → Devices & Sessions

✓ Rule 8: Enable SIM Lock to prevent SIM cloning.

Settings → Security → SIM Lock

4. If You Got Scammed — Do This IMMEDIATELY

Act within minutes — recovery chances drop rapidly.

Step 1 — Call 1930 (National Cyber Fraud Helpline)

Tell them:

“I need to file a UPI Fraud Transaction Hold.”

Step 2 — Contact your bank immediately

Ask them to block the fraudulent transaction.

Step 3 — File complaint at cybercrime.gov.in

https://cybercrime.gov.in

Step 4 — Save all screenshots

Step 5 — Block and report scammer’s UPI ID

5. Messages Banks NEVER Send

Banks will NEVER ask for:

  • UPI PIN
  • OTP
  • CVV
  • QR scan for refund
  • “Activation fee”
