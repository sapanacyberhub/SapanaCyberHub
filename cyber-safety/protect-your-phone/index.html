Protect Your Phone From Hackers — Deep Cyber Safety Guide (2025)

SapanaCyberHub Cyber Safety Guide

Your smartphone carries WhatsApp, banking, photos, identity and your entire digital life. This guide teaches you how to **detect spyware**, **remove hidden stalker apps**, **stop hackers**, and **protect UPI & OTPs**.

On this page: Signs your phone is hacked Quick 10-minute protection checklist Deep detection & removal steps Protect WhatsApp, UPI & Email Long-term protection If hacked — recovery steps Where to report

Signs your phone may be hacked

  • Phone heats up while not being used
  • Fast battery drain
  • Unknown apps visible in settings
  • OTP messages you didn’t request
  • WhatsApp logs out unexpectedly
  • Mysterious bank SMS alerts
  • High background data usage

Quick 10-minute protection checklist

  • Enable strong lockscreen password
  • Update OS + all apps
  • Delete suspicious apps immediately
  • Enable 2FA on Gmail, WhatsApp, Instagram
  • Check login activity on Google/Apple
  • Scan phone (Android) with Malwarebytes
  • Remove unknown VPN/profiles

Deep detection & removal steps

1. Check installed apps

Android: Settings → Apps → Installed apps  
iPhone: Home screen + Settings → General → Profiles

2. Look for hidden spyware names

System Service  
Update Engine  
Device Health  
Browser Manager  
Network Optimizer

3. Remove Device Admin Apps (Android)

Settings → Security → Device Admin Apps

4. Remove unknown profiles (iPhone)

Settings → General → VPN & Device Management

5. Scan with trusted anti-spyware tools

Use Malwarebytes or Bitdefender.

6. Check Google login activity

https://myaccount.google.com/device-activity

7. Check WhatsApp linked devices

WhatsApp → Settings → Linked Devices

8. Factory reset if infection is deep

Android: Erase all data  
iPhone: Erase All Content

Protect WhatsApp, UPI & Email

WhatsApp

  • Enable two-step verification
  • Clear unknown linked devices

Email

  • Change password from safe device
  • Enable authenticator app
  • Remove unknown recovery emails

UPI / Banking

  • Lock apps with biometrics
  • Remove unknown UPI devices
  • Block UPI if suspicious activity occurs

Long-term protection (SapanaCyberHub Recommended)

  1. Install apps only from Play Store/App Store
  2. Disable “Install unknown apps”
  3. Use password manager
  4. Enable 2FA everywhere
  5. Limit permissions (camera/mic/location)
  6. Take regular encrypted backups

If hacked — recovery steps

  1. Change all passwords from a clean device
  2. Backup important files
  3. Reset phone
  4. Block UPI temporarily
  5. Report to cyber cell
