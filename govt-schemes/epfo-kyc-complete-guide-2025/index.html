Incomplete KYC is the #1 reason for PF delays. This guide ensures you complete it without rejection.

What is EPFO KYC?

EPFO KYC is the identity verification process that links your Aadhaar, PAN, bank account and phone number with your UAN. Without KYC, PF withdrawal, PF transfer, and claim approval cannot happen.

KYC Components

  • Aadhaar: Identity verification & OTP
  • PAN: For tax deduction
  • Bank Account: For direct PF deposit
  • Mobile Number: Must match Aadhaar

Benefits of Completing KYC

  • Instant claim approval
  • Faster PF withdrawal
  • No employer dependency
  • No TDS penalties
  • Secure verification

How to Check Your KYC Status

  1. Login to the EPFO Portal
  2. Go to Manage → KYC
  3. View approved or pending status

How to Update EPFO KYC

  1. Click Here to update Kyc
  2. Enter Aadhaar, PAN, bank details
  3. Submit for employer approval
  4. Complete Aadhaar OTP
  5. Go to Manage → KYC
