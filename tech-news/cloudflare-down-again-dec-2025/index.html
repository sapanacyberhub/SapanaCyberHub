Cloudflare Down *Again* — Full Report & Impact (Dec 5, 2025)

By Sapana · Dec 5, 2025 · SapanaCyberHub

Cloudflare suffered a global outage on Dec 5, 2025, causing 500 errors and downtime across major apps and trading platforms. Read the full report, timeline, impact, and recovery steps for site owners.

Site owner recovery steps

What happened — quick summary

On Dec 5, 2025, Cloudflare experienced a global service disruption that caused many websites and apps to return 500 Internal Server Error responses, broken logins, and partial downtime across services that rely on Cloudflare's CDN, DNS, and edge network.

  • Widespread 5xx errors and inaccessible sites
  • Issues began during scheduled maintenance at a Cloudflare data center
  • Cloudflare reported an internal service degradation — not a cyberattack

Who and what was affected

Because Cloudflare provides CDN, DNS, firewall, and edge routing services, platforms that rely on those features were impacted. Global services and many Indian trading platforms reported interruptions.

  • Global platforms: Zoom, Canva, LinkedIn, Discord, QuillBot, Medium
  • India / fintech: Zerodha (Kite), Groww, Angel One, Upstox — users reported login and trade placement issues

Root cause (official)

Cloudflare's status updates indicated the issue was caused by maintenance that led to incorrect traffic routing and internal service load spikes. That caused components to return errors and propagate failures across the network. Cloudflare clarified it was a technical maintenance failure, not a malicious incident.

Actionable steps for website owners (what to do now)

  1. Check Cloudflare status: cloudflarestatus.com
  2. Don't change DNS settings immediately. Wait until Cloudflare stability is confirmed to avoid propagation issues.
  3. Enable server-side caching to serve static pages while the CDN recovers.
  4. Consider redundancy: use multi-DNS (example: Cloudflare + AWS Route53) and origin fallback options.
  5. Monitor logs and rollback risky changes until full recovery.

Conclusion — centralization risk & next steps

The Dec 5 outage is a strong reminder of the internet's reliance on a few core providers. While Cloudflare remains indispensable for security and speed, businesses must plan for redundancy and better failover to reduce the impact of such outages.
