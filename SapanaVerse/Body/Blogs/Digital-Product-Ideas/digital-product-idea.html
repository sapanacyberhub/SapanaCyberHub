Quick FAQ

Which product should I start with?

Pick the fastest path: if you write, do an eBook; if you design, ship templates; if you teach, record a short course.

How do I price digital products?

Start with value tiers: ₹299 for a starter pack, ₹799 for a pro bundle, ₹1499 for a premium course with extras.

How can AI help?

Use AI for research, outlines, draft polishing, graphics, and repurposing into social posts — keep your voice in edits.
