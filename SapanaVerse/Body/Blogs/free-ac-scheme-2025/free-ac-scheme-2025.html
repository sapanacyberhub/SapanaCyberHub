Free ACs for Low-Income Families? The Truth Behind India’s Viral ‘Summer Relief Scheme’

A full fact-check on the viral claim spreading across social media.

Why This News Suddenly Went Viral

Across India, heatwaves are breaking records. Temperatures are hitting 47°C+, and in this situation a viral headline started trending on social media:

“Government to provide Free ACs under Summer Relief Scheme 2025.”

Millions searched online: “Free AC kaise milega?”, “Apply link?”, “Is this real?” But what’s the truth?

Is the Free AC Scheme Real?

No. After checking official government portals, PIB, MyGov, and state websites, the truth is clear:

❌ No official scheme exists that provides free ACs to the public.
  • No announcement by the Ministry of Power
  • No state notification
  • No official subsidy document

Then What Programs Actually Exist?

Even though Free AC isn’t real, government has multiple heat-relief & energy-saving programs:

1. PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

Solar rooftop system — free electricity — AC use becomes cheap.

2. Energy Efficiency Subsidy

Discounts on high-efficiency appliances for BPL families.

3. Heat-Action Relief Plans

Cooling centers, water booths, and temporary heat shelters.

Why Fake News Like This Goes Viral

  • Extreme heatwave fear
  • People desperately want cooling relief
  • Creators make clickbait thumbnails
  • Lack of fact-checking

Beware of Fake “Free AC Registration” Sites

If any site asks for Aadhaar, OTP, bank details, or ₹10–₹199 registration fee:

⚠️ It is 100% fake and a scam.

Realistic Cooling Help Government May Launch in Future

  • AC subsidy for low-income families
  • Public cooling zones
  • Affordable AC rental program
  • Solar AC incentive

Final Fact-Check Summary

  • Free AC Scheme? ❌ Fake
  • Summer Relief Scheme? ❌ Not official
  • Heat-relief programs? ✔ Yes
  • Subsidies exist? ✔ Some
  • WhatsApp links? ❌ Scam

India may introduce real cooling schemes in future, but as of now, the viral “Free AC Scheme” is completely fake.
