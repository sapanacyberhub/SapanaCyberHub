New Android Tricks 2025 — Hidden Features You Never Knew

Updated on: 26 November 2025 • 7:45 AM IST • Android Tips & Tricks

Android keeps getting smarter every year, but most users still use it like a basic phone. In this 2025 guide, you’ll learn 15 powerful Android tricks and hidden features that can:

  • Boost your phone speed and smoothness
  • Improve battery backup
  • Increase privacy and security
  • Make daily tasks faster and easier
Note: Names of settings may change slightly depending on your Android brand (Samsung, Realme, Redmi, OnePlus, etc.), but the features are similar.

1. Supercharged Quick Settings Panel

Swipe down twice from the top to open the full Quick Settings panel. In Android 13+ and 14+ you can:

  • Reorder tiles (Wi-Fi, Mobile Data, Hotspot, Dark Mode, Screen Record, etc.)
  • Add tiles like Battery Saver, Screen Cast, and Focus Mode
  • Long-press any tile to open its full setting instantly

2. Privacy Dashboard & Live Indicators

Go to Settings → Security & Privacy → Privacy Dashboard. Here you can see which apps recently used your camera, microphone, location.

When any app uses them, a small colored dot appears in the status bar. If you see it randomly, open the dashboard — maybe a bad app is spying on you.

3. Secure Clipboard Protection

Android 13+ now clears clipboard history automatically after a short time. This protects sensitive data like passwords, OTPs and bank details.

You can also block clipboard access for specific apps from Settings → Privacy → Clipboard access.

Ad Placement

4. Per-App Language Control

Want YouTube in English but WhatsApp in Hindi? Go to Settings → System → Languages → App Languages and select a different language for each app.

5. One-Handed Mode for Big Screens

On big phones, enable one-handed mode:

  • Settings → System → Gestures → One-handed mode
  • After that, swipe down on the bottom edge to shrink the screen.

6. Notification History (See Cleared Alerts)

If you accidentally clear notifications, enable: Settings → Notifications → Notification history.

Now you can see all notifications from the last 24 hours.

7. Smart Text Selection Everywhere

Long-press text anywhere (Chrome, WhatsApp, apps) and you’ll see extra options like: Search, Maps, Call, Translate. This saves a lot of time copying and switching between apps.

8. Advanced Screenshot & Screen Recording

Most modern Android phones now support:

  • Scrolling screenshots for long pages
  • Built-in screen recorder with internal audio
  • Markup tools to draw, blur or highlight on screenshots

9. Game Mode & Performance Boost

Gamers should enable Game Mode / Game Dashboard (name depends on phone). You can:

  • Block calls & notifications while gaming
  • Show FPS counter and performance overlay
  • Choose “Performance” or “Battery Saver” mode for each game

10. Reduce Animations for Speed

To make your phone feel faster:

  1. Enable developer options: Settings → About phone → Tap “Build number” 7 times
  2. Go to Settings → System → Developer options
  3. Set the three animation scales (window, transition, animator) to 0.5x or Off

Your phone will feel almost instant.

11. Adaptive Battery & Sleep Apps

Use Adaptive Battery and sleep/deep-sleep options to restrict apps that you rarely use. This can easily add 1–2 hours of extra battery per day.

12. Auto Rules for DND & Focus

Under Digital Wellbeing → Focus Mode / Do Not Disturb, create rules like:

  • Block social media during study or work time
  • Allow only family calls at night

13. Fast File Sharing with Nearby Share

Nearby Share is like AirDrop for Android:

  • Enable from Quick Settings → Nearby Share
  • Share large files instantly with nearby Android or Chromebook devices

14. Useful Hidden Dialer Codes

On many devices, dialer codes like *#06# show IMEI, and some codes open testing menus. Use them carefully and never change options you don’t understand.

15. Built-in Safety & Emergency Features

Modern Android has Safety & Emergency options:

  • Emergency SOS (press power button 5 times on many phones)
  • Share live location with trusted contacts
  • Store medical information on the lock screen

Final Thoughts

Android 2025 is far more powerful than most people realize. If you explore these hidden tricks and settings, your phone will feel newer, faster and more secure — without installing any fake “booster” apps.

Save this page and try 2–3 tricks every day. Also, don’t forget to check our other Android and cybersecurity guides below.
