Quick Verdict

If you need a phone that lasts for days, has flagship‑style display smoothness and packs competitive gaming performance at a budget price, the realme P4x 5G is an excellent pick. Its 7000mAh battery and VC cooling make it a top choice in the ₹15k–₹20k bracket.

Design & Build — Practical, Durable, Lightweight

The P4x uses a reinforced polycarbonate back with Armorshell protection and an internal aluminum frame for rigidity. While the phone doesn’t chase glass‑sandwich premium looks, Realme prioritizes durability and thermal management — crucial when you have a 7000mAh battery inside.

Display & Multimedia

The 144Hz Sunlight Display is a highlight. With a high touch‑sampling rate and 1000 nits peak brightness, it stays readable outdoors while offering buttery scrolls and reduced motion blur. Streamers and gamers will appreciate the smoother UI and high frame‑rate gameplay experience.

Performance & Gaming

MediaTek Dimensity 7400 Ultra is a balanced mid‑range SoC with efficient cores and a competent GPU. Combined with UFS 3.1 storage and Realme’s VC cooling, the P4x can sustain longer gaming sessions at stable FPS levels. Expect BGMI / COD Mobile to run comfortably at medium‑high settings.

Estimated gaming: BGMI 60FPS (Smooth), COD Mobile 60–90FPS depending on settings

Battery Life & Charging

7000mAh battery means exceptional standby and multi‑day usage for light users. Heavy users and gamers will still find it lasts a full day easily. 45W charging is not the fastest on paper, but it provides a practical balance between charge speed and battery health.

Camera Performance — What to Expect

The 50MP main sensor handles daylight shots very well for social sharing; low‑light performance improves with multi‑frame processing and Night Mode. The P4x is not marketed as a camera champion — expect reliable everyday shots rather than flagship‑grade results.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Massive 7000mAh battery for multi‑day use
  • 144Hz display with good brightness
  • VC vapor chamber cooling — great for sustained loads
  • Value pricing for performance and battery

Cons

  • Average camera setup compared to flagship phones
  • Polycarbonate back instead of glass (tradeoff for durability)
  • 45W charging is fast but competitors offer faster speeds

Price & Where to Buy

Launch offers often include bank discounts and exchange deals — check Realme’s official store and major retailers (Flipkart, Amazon) for day‑one offers. Prices vary across regions; the Indian launch targets under ₹18k for base models.

Final Verdict

The realme P4x 5G is a practical, cleverly engineered phone for users who prioritize battery life and consistent performance. Realme’s combination of a large battery, efficient chipset, and cool‑down systems makes it a strong competitor in its segment. If long battery life and smooth display are your priorities — this phone should be at the top of your shortlist.
