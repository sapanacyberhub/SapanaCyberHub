1. What is PMAY-G?

Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana–Gramin (PMAY-G) is the flagship rural housing scheme of the Government of India. Its goal is to provide safe pucca houses with basic facilities to poor and homeless families living in villages.

Under the scheme, eligible households receive financial assistance to construct a permanent house with:

  • Strong walls and RCC or other permanent roof
  • Separate room, kitchen and living area (as per local design)
  • Toilet facility
  • Access to drinking water, electricity and LPG connection through convergence with other schemes

PMAY-G replaces the old Indira Awaas Yojana and brings in more transparency, targeting and digital tracking.

2. PMAY-G 2025 Latest Updates

Here are some key highlights for PMAY-G in 2024–2025:

✅ Awaas+ Survey to Cover Left-Out Families

Families who were not included in the original SECC 2011 data are being captured through the Awaas+ Survey. This helps add eligible households who still live in kutcha or semi-kutcha houses.

✅ Faster Verification & Online Monitoring

Verification at Gram Sabha, block and district level is now supported through IT platforms like AwaasSoft. This makes the process faster and more transparent.

✅ Fund Assistance with Convergence

Along with core house assistance under PMAY-G, beneficiaries may also get:

  • Toilet support under Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM-G)
  • Wage labour support under MGNREGA
  • Electricity connection under Saubhagya / DDUGJY
  • LPG connection under PM Ujjwala Yojana
  • Drinking water under Jal Jeevan Mission

3. PMAY-G 2025 Eligibility Criteria

Beneficiaries are selected on the basis of housing deprivation parameters from SECC 2011 and updated surveys. You may be eligible if your family:

  • Lives in a rural area
  • Lives in a kutcha house (temporary structure) or in a house with ≤ 2 rooms
  • Does not own any pucca house anywhere in India
  • Is homeless or living in dilapidated housing
  • Is a woman-headed household
  • Includes disabled members, SC/ST, landless labourers, or other vulnerable groups
  • Has no adult earning member (in some categories)

Who is generally NOT eligible?

  • Families owning a pucca house (roof and walls made of permanent material)
  • Households where a member has a government job or pays income tax
  • Families with high-value assets (such as four-wheeler, large landholding etc., as per local criteria)
Tip: Even if your name is missing from earlier lists, you can request inclusion during the Awaas+ survey through your Gram Panchayat.

4. Financial Assistance & Benefits under PMAY-G

The assistance amount under PMAY-G is shared between the Central and State governments. The basic support for constructing a house is:

Location House Assistance Toilet (SBM-G) MGNREGA Wage Support Approx. Total Support
Plain Areas ₹1.20 lakh ₹12,000 Up to ₹18,000 ≈ ₹1.50 lakh
Hilly / Difficult Areas ₹1.30 lakh ₹12,000 Up to ₹18,000 ≈ ₹1.60 lakh

In addition, beneficiaries benefit from converged schemes for electricity, LPG and water connection. Funds are released in 3–4 installments directly to the beneficiary’s bank account based on construction progress.

5. How to Apply for PMAY-G 2025 (Step-by-Step)

In most cases, applications are facilitated through the Gram Panchayat rather than individuals applying directly like online forms for other schemes. However, knowing the process helps you follow it correctly.

Step 1 – Check if your village is covered

Visit the official portal pmayg.nic.in or contact your Gram Panchayat to confirm coverage and current survey status.

Step 2 – Participate in SECC / Awaas+ Survey

Survey teams or Panchayat officials collect information such as housing condition, family details and socio-economic status. Make sure that:

  • Your details are correctly recorded
  • Your house type is correctly shown as kutcha / dilapidated
  • Aadhaar and bank account details are provided

Step 3 – Gram Sabha Verification

The list of potential beneficiaries is placed before the Gram Sabha for approval. This ensures community-level transparency.

Step 4 – Sanction of House

After verification at block and district level, the approved beneficiary receives an official sanction order. Details are updated in the AwaasSoft portal.

Step 5 – Fund Release & Construction

Funds are credited in installments. You are expected to start construction soon after receiving the first installment and complete within the given period, following the technical design norms.

6. Documents Required for PMAY-G

Exact list may differ slightly by state, but usually includes:

  • Aadhaar Card of head of family
  • Ration Card / Family ID
  • Bank Account Passbook (linked with Aadhaar)
  • Job Card (MGNREGA), if available
  • Land ownership papers or proof of land availability
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Caste certificate (SC/ST or other categories, if applicable)
  • Disability certificate, if claiming disability-based priority
  • Mobile number for receiving SMS updates

7. How to Check PMAY-G Beneficiary List & Status

You can check if your name is on the beneficiary list or track house status online.

Method 1 – Using Registration Number

  1. Go to the official website: pmayg.nic.in
  2. Click on AwaasSoft → Stakeholders → IAY/PMAYG Beneficiary.
  3. Enter your Registration Number.
  4. Your details, sanction status and installment release information will appear.

Method 2 – Search by Name & Other Details

  1. If you don’t know the registration number, choose the option to search by state, district, block, panchayat and name.
  2. Fill the required fields and submit.
  3. The site will display a list of matching beneficiaries with details.

8. What is Awaas+ Survey 2025?

Awaas+ is a special survey mechanism introduced to identify those rural households who were left out from earlier data but are genuinely in need of a house.

If you live in a kutcha house and your name is not in PMAY-G lists, you should:

  • Contact your Gram Panchayat as soon as the Awaas+ survey is active in your area
  • Provide correct details, Aadhaar and bank account information
  • Ensure your house condition is properly documented by the survey team

This greatly increases your chances of inclusion in future sanction lists.

9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on PMAY-G 2025

Q1. What is PMAY-G 2025 in simple words?

PMAY-G is a government scheme that helps poor rural families get money to build a pucca house. In 2025, the scheme continues with stronger digital monitoring and fresh surveys to cover left-out families.

Q2. Can I apply online myself from home?

Most of the process goes through the Gram Panchayat and survey teams. While some details are filled online in the portal, it is usually done by authorized officials. You should stay in touch with your Panchayat to ensure your inclusion.

Q3. How many installments are given?

Typically, the assistance is released in 3–4 installments linked to construction stages: sanction, plinth level, roof level and completion. Exact pattern may vary by state.

Q4. Can I build a bigger house than the amount provided?

Yes. The grant amount is a minimum support. You can use your savings, loans or other support to build a larger or better-designed house.

Q5. My name is still not in the list. What should I do?

Meet your Gram Panchayat, ask about the next Awaas+ survey or inclusion process, and keep your documents ready. You can also check status on the PMAY-G website regularly.
