Event Title

Join the Neon Night Vibes event to immerse yourself in a futuristic music experience. Listen to curated tracks, accumulate listening hours, and climb the leaderboard for a chance to win real rewards.

The total prize pool for this event is ₹1,000 distributed among the top 10 listeners.

  • Accumulate listening hours to climb the leaderboard.

  • Additional rewards are available for the top 1000 listeners, distributed in the form of Listen Coins.
